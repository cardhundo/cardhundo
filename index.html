Find My Car

Six choices are used as filters first and ranking signals second. A car that fails a hard requirement is not allowed to outrank a true match.

Budget is treated as a range. Seats are treated as a minimum. Fuel, body type and transmission are hard filters. Efficiency is only used where the database contains a comparable mileage value; EVs use official range.
